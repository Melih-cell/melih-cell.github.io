Seri — Account & Data Deletion

Seri: Habit Tracker & Focus works offline-first without requiring an account. If you opted into Cloud Sync, Shared Campfires, or Weekly Leagues, you can permanently delete your local and cloud data at any time.

1. Instant On-Device Data Deletion

  1. Open Seri on your Android device.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Reset / Delete All Data to immediately wipe all local habits, streaks, journal notes, and preferences.
  4. If signed into optional Cloud Sync, sign out and disable Cloud Sync in the Cloud Account card.

2. Permanent Cloud Account & Server Data Deletion Request

To permanently delete your optional cloud account and all server-side records (even if you already uninstalled the app), send an email from the address associated with your Seri Cloud Account to melihdokuzlar71@gmail.com with the subject Seri Account & Data Deletion Request.

Verified deletion requests are completed permanently within 72 hours.

3. Data Permanently Deleted

  • Optional Cloud Authentication account (email address and user ID)
  • Encrypted cloud backup snapshots (habits, check-ins, focus logs, journal entries, and settings)
  • Optional social profile (display name, avatar emoji, weekly league stats, and partner/campfire room memberships)
  • Registered Expo push notification token used for campfire reactions/nudges

Retention note: No personal habit names, journal notes, or email addresses are retained after account deletion. Purchase receipts remain managed solely by Google Play Billing under Google's retention policies.
