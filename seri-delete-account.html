Seri — Account & Data Deletion

Seri: Habit Tracker & Focus works offline-first without requiring an account. If you opted into Cloud Sync, Shared Campfires, or Weekly Leagues, you can permanently delete your local and cloud data at any time.

1. Instant In-App Permanent Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open Seri on your Android device and go to the Settings tab.
  2. In the Cloud Account card, tap Permanently Delete Account & Cloud Data and confirm to immediately erase your Supabase authentication account, cloud backups, weekly league scores, partner streaks, campfire memberships, AI usage logs, and push tokens.
  3. To wipe local on-device records as well, tap Reset / Delete All Data at the bottom of Settings.

2. Web / Email Account & Server Data Deletion Request

If you already uninstalled the app, send an email from the address associated with your Seri Cloud Account to melihdokuzlar71@gmail.com with the subject Seri Account & Data Deletion Request.

Verified deletion requests are completed permanently within 72 hours.

3. Data Permanently Deleted

  • Supabase Cloud Authentication account (email address and user ID)
  • Encrypted cloud backup snapshots (habits, check-ins, focus logs, journal entries, and settings)
  • Optional social profile (display name, avatar emoji, weekly league stats, and partner/campfire room memberships)
  • AI Coach daily usage counters, content reports, and registered Expo push notification token

Billing note: No personal habit names, journal notes, or email addresses are retained after account deletion. Active subscriptions are managed by Google Play and are not automatically cancelled when deleting your account — cancel anytime in Google Play Subscriptions.

Türkçe Özet — Seri Hesap ve Veri Silme

Seri: Alışkanlık Takip & Odak hesap açmadan çevrimdışı çalışır. İsteğe bağlı Supabase Bulut Hesabınızı ve tüm sunucu verilerinizi uygulama içinden Ayarlar → Bulut Hesabı → Hesabımı ve Bulut Verilerimi Kalıcı Sil düğmesiyle anında silebilirsiniz. Cihazdaki yerel kayıtlar için Ayarlar → Tüm Verileri Sıfırla adımını kullanabilirsiniz.

Uygulamayı kaldırdıysanız kayıtlı e-posta adresinizden melihdokuzlar71@gmail.com adresine "Seri Hesap ve Veri Silme Talebi" başlığıyla yazmanız yeterlidir. Tüm sunucu verileriniz en geç 72 saat içinde kalıcı olarak silinir.
