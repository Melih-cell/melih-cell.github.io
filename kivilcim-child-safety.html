Vela: Meet & Real Chat — Child Safety Standards (CSAE & CSAM)

App Name: Vela: Meet & Real Chat (com.melih.kivilcim) • Developer: Veloce Labs • Child Safety Contact: melihdokuzlar71@gmail.com

Strict Zero-Tolerance Statement: Vela: Meet & Real Chat, published by Veloce Labs on Google Play, enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), grooming, minor solicitation, or any account created by or depicting any person under 18 years of age.

1. Strict 18+ Adult-Only Eligibility

  • Vela: Meet & Real Chat is exclusively for adults aged 18 and older. Every user must verify their date of birth (18+) and explicitly accept our Terms & Child Safety Rules during registration, enforced both on-device and in Cloud Firestore Security Rules (age >= 18 and acceptedTermsAndAge18 == true).
  • Any profile or account suspected of involving, depicting, or soliciting minors is immediately suspended, hidden from all discovery pools, and permanently deleted.

2. In-App Safety Reporting & Automated CSAE Prevention

  • In-App Child Safety & Abuse Reporting: Users can report child safety concerns, suspected underage profiles, or CSAE violations directly inside the app via the one-tap Report & Block mechanism present on every profile card, detail sheet, and chat screen (selecting the dedicated Child Safety / CSAE reason option).
  • Instant Blocking & Automated Moderation: Blocking or reporting immediately removes the reported user from discovery, likes, and messaging at the database level. Automated content screening detects CSAE/underage solicitation indicators, and multi-report escalation automatically isolates flagged accounts.

3. Legal Compliance & Reporting to Regional / National Authorities

Veloce Labs complies with all applicable child protection laws and reports confirmed CSAM or CSAE incidents without delay to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant regional and national law enforcement authorities, preserving de-identified audit records strictly as required by law.

4. Published Child Safety Point of Contact (Veloce Labs)

To report any child safety concern, suspected underage user, or CSAE violation on Vela: Meet & Real Chat, contact our designated Veloce Labs Child Safety Officer immediately at: melihdokuzlar71@gmail.com.
