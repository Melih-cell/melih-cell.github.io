Vela — Child Safety Standards (CSAE & CSAM)

Strict Zero-Tolerance Statement: Vela, developed by Veloce Studio, strictly prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), grooming, solicitation of minors, or any account created by or depicting anyone under the age of 18.

1. Strict 18+ Adult Eligibility

  • Every user must explicitly confirm they are at least 18 years old during registration, enforced both in the Android client and in Cloud Firestore Security Rules (age >= 18 and acceptedTermsAndAge18 == true).
  • Accounts suspected of belonging to minors are immediately suspended and permanently deleted.

2. Automated Prevention & In-App Safety Controls

  • Automated Content Filtering: Profiles and chat messages are automatically screened for CSAE/underage solicitation indicators, harassment, and scam patterns.
  • One-Tap In-App Reporting & Blocking: Every profile card, detail view, and chat conversation provides an immediate Report & Block action. Blocked users are instantly removed from discovery, likes, and messaging at the database level.
  • Multi-Report Escalation: Accounts reported by 3 or more distinct users are automatically escalated for urgent moderation review and hidden from discovery.

3. Legal Compliance & Reporting to Authorities

Veloce Studio complies with all applicable child safety laws and reports confirmed CSAM or CSAE incidents to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant regional law enforcement authorities without delay.

4. Dedicated Child Safety Point of Contact

To report any child safety concern, suspected underage user, or CSAE violation on Vela, contact our Child Safety Officer immediately at: melihdokuzlar198@gmail.com.
