Vela: Meet & Real Chat — Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your Vela: Meet & Real Chat account and all associated personal data at any time either directly inside the app or via web request.

1. Instant In-App Account Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open Vela on your Android device and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile (Profilim) tab and tap the Settings (⚙️) button.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account Permanently (Hesabımı Kalıcı Olarak Sil).
  4. Confirm deletion. Your account and cloud data are permanently removed immediately.

2. Web / Email Deletion Request (If You Uninstalled the App)

If you no longer have access to the app, send an email from the address registered to your account to melihdokuzlar71@gmail.com with the subject Vela Account Deletion Request. We process all verified requests within 72 hours.

3. Data Permanently Deleted

  • Firebase Authentication account and login credentials
  • Public profile (users_public) and private settings/email/push token (users_private)
  • All uploaded profile photos (user_photos)
  • Active matches, chat messages, date invites, and AAC voice notes (matches, messages, voice_blobs)
  • Likes and local cached files

Retention note: De-identified abuse/safety reports submitted for legal or CSAE compliance may be retained only as required by applicable law. No profile, photo, or private email is retained after deletion.
