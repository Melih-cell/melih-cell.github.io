1. Eligibility & Community Rules (UGC)

Sparka / Kıvılcım is strictly for adults aged 18+. Users must not post illegal content, hate speech, harassment, impersonation, financial/IBAN scams, or non-consensual explicit material. Violations result in immediate account removal.

2. Data We Collect & How It Is Protected

  • Account & Profile Data: Email address (kept strictly private in owner-only storage), display name, age (18+), gender/preferences, bio, interests, and up to 4 compressed profile photos.
  • Approximate Location (~3 km Privacy Grid): If you opt into approximate city detection, coordinates are snapped to an approximate ~3 km grid on-device. We never collect or share exact street-level GPS coordinates.
  • On-Device Live Face Verification: Optional selfie verification runs 100% locally on your device using Google ML Kit Face Detection. Camera frames and facial geometry are never uploaded to servers or shared with third parties.
  • Chat & Voice Notes: Text messages, date invites, and optional 15-second AAC voice clips are accessible only to the two matched participants and are automatically cleaned up upon unmatch, message deletion, or account deletion.
  • Push Notifications: FCM tokens are stored strictly in owner-only private records to deliver match and message alerts.
  • Advertising & Purchases: Free accounts may see AdMob interstitial/rewarded ads. Gold VIP subscriptions and lifetime upgrades are processed securely by Google Play Billing and remove all ads.

3. Your Rights (GDPR / KVKK) & Account Deletion

You may edit your profile, manage visibility (Incognito), block/unblock users, or permanently delete your account and all data at any time via Account Deletion Portal or inside Profile → Settings.
