Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026 • Developer: Veloce Labs (Melih Dokuzlar)

This Privacy Policy applies to all live mobile applications and games published by Veloce Labs on Google Play, including Seri: Habit Tracker & Focus, Wallify: HD & 4K Wallpapers, LumiaWalls: AMOLED & 8K Stüdyo, Bilgi Arenası: Bilgi Yarışması, Kızıl Katedral: Aksiyon RPG, and SnapMind: Image to Text & OCR.

1. Information Collection and Use

Most app data (such as preferences, game progress, habits, notes, OCR text recognition via on-device Google ML Kit, and custom wallpapers) is stored locally on your device. We do not sell your personal information.

  • Advertising Identifiers: Free-tier versions of our apps use Google AdMob to display banners, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), coarse location, and diagnostics for ad serving, fraud prevention, and consent management (Google UMP / TCF v2.2).
  • In-App Purchases: All subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We never access or store your credit card or banking details.
  • Crash & Analytics Data: Some apps use Google Play Services / Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics to diagnose crashes and improve stability.

2. Permissions

  • Photos / Media / Camera (Optional): Used strictly on-device when you choose an image for OCR scanning (SnapMind) or apply/save a wallpaper (Wallify, LumiaWalls).
  • Internet & Billing: Used to load content, process Google Play purchases, and display advertisements.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Local application data can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the application from your device. If an app provides optional cloud leaderboard or profile features, you may request deletion of associated records at any time by emailing melihdokuzlar71@gmail.com.

4. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Users can reset or opt out of personalized advertising in their Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
